What Can You See While Diving in Agatti?

The marine ecosystem surrounding Agatti Island ranks among the richest and best preserved in the Indian Ocean. Here is what awaits beneath the surface:

Pristine Habitat

Vibrant Coral Formations

Explore vast fields of healthy table corals, massive brain coral structures, swaying gorgonian sea fans, and natural submarine arches bathed in golden sunlight.

Intact, protected coral reefs
Frequent Sightings

Hawksbill & Green Sea Turtles

Agatti’s calm lagoons are prime grazing grounds for resident sea turtles. Watch them glide effortlessly over seagrass meadows and resting along coral ledges.

Gentle, wild turtle encounters
Colourful Life

Colourful Tropical Reef Fish

Swim alongside thousands of clownfish (Nemo) peeking from anemones, parrotfish grazing on corals, school of blue tangs, bannerfish, and lionfish.

Over 300+ tropical fish species
Deep Channels

Eagle Rays & Pelagic Wonders

In outer lagoon drop-offs, observe magnificent spotted eagle rays flying past, blue-spotted stingrays resting on white sandy channels, and harmless juvenile reef sharks.

Outer atoll channel dive spots

First Time Diving? Here's How Easy It Is

You do not need prior scuba experience or even swimming skills for introductory lagoon dives. Our patient step-by-step approach ensures you feel completely safe and confident.

Equipment Fitting & Safety Briefing

We fit you with comfortable masks, fins, and BCDs, explaining breathing techniques and easy hand signals in simple words.

2

When Is the Best Time for Scuba Diving in Agatti?

The prime diving season in Agatti Island runs from October through May. During these months, the Arabian Sea is extraordinarily calm, sunny weather prevails, and underwater visibility peaks up to 35 meters. Heavy monsoon swells pause operations between June and September for your safety.
