Complete Lakshadweep Scuba Diving Guide: Best Season, Entry Permits, Marine Life & PADI Courses

Everything You Need to Know to Plan Your Agatti Island Scuba Adventure and Coral Reef Safari
October 12, 2026 Lak Sea Adventures Team 8 Min Read Lakshadweep Guide

Lakshadweep is India's crown jewel for tropical scuba diving. An archipelago of 36 islands and atolls in the Arabian Sea, it offers an untouched coral wonderland comparable to the Maldives. However, planning a scuba trip to Lakshadweep requires understanding key logistics: dive seasons, mandatory entry permits, flight connections, and local marine life.

This comprehensive guide from Lak Sea Adventures covers everything you need to know to plan a seamless, world-class diving adventure in Agatti Island and surrounding atolls.

When to Dive: Best Seasons & Weather Conditions

Understanding the weather cycle ensures you catch Lakshadweep when visibility and sea conditions are at their absolute peak:

  • Peak Season (November to April): This is Lakshadweep's dry window. Seas are calm, rainfall is minimal, and underwater visibility consistently exceeds 20 to 30 meters. December through March offers the calmest conditions for both beginner courses and deep wall dives.
  • Water Temperature: A comfortable 27°C to 30°C year-round. A 3mm wetsuit or simple rash guard is sufficient for most divers.
  • Best Time of Day: Morning dives between 7:00 AM and 9:30 AM offer the calmest waters, lightest breezes, and optimal ambient sunlight for underwater photography.
  • Monsoon Season (June to September): Strong southwest monsoons bring rough seas and heavy winds. Most dive operations pause boat departures during this period.
Clear waters, calm morning seas, and shallow reefs make Agatti Island the premier diving hub of Lakshadweep.

Entry Permits & Travel Logistics: How to Get to Agatti

Lakshadweep is an ecologically protected union territory. Every visitor—Indian or international—requires an official Entry Permit issued by the Lakshadweep Administration before boarding their flight.

We Handle Your Entry Permit Hassle-Free

When you book your diving or resort package with Lak Sea Adventures, our team manages your entire permit application: document collection, processing, and administrative approvals. You avoid bureaucratic headaches and focus entirely on your vacation.

Flights: Agatti Island (AGX) hosts the only civilian airport in Lakshadweep, with daily flights connecting directly from Kochi (COK). Once you touch down in Agatti, our team meets you at the airport and handles your transfers directly to your accommodation and dive center.

Marine Life You Will Actually Encounter in Agatti

Agatti's coral reefs are biodiversity hotspots teeming with marine encounters on almost every dive:

  • Sea Turtles: Resident green turtles and hawksbills populate the lagoons and outer reefs.
  • Reef Sharks: Whitetip reef sharks resting under coral ledges and grey reef sharks patrolling deep channel walls.
  • Rays: Spotted eagle rays and mobula rays gliding effortlessly through channel currents.
  • Pelagic Schools: Thousands of yellowback fusiliers, snappers, jacks, and barracudas schooling along the drop-offs.
  • Nocturnal Magic: Moray eels, hunting octopus, squid, and glowing bioluminescent plankton on guided night dives.

Available PADI Certification & Specialty Courses

Beyond our standard Open Water and Advanced courses, Lak Sea Adventures offers high-value specialty certifications:

  • PADI Deep Diver: Safely extend your exploration ceiling down to 40 meters.
  • PADI Enriched Air (Nitrox): Enjoy longer no-decompression limits and shorter surface intervals.
  • PADI Night Diver: Witness coral polyps feeding and nocturnal hunters on Agatti's "Wall of Wonder."
  • PADI Peak Performance Buoyancy: Perfect your trim, weight distribution, and air consumption.
  • PADI Underwater Photography: Capture magazine-quality reef photos under professional guidance.
