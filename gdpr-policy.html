Effective Date: 1 August 2025. At Lak Sea Adventures, we are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This GDPR Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU) 2016/679. This policy applies to visitors from the European Union (EU) and European Economic Area (EEA) who access our website at https://lakseadventures.com/.

1. Scope of This Policy

This GDPR Policy explains:

  • What personal data we collect from you
  • How we collect and use your data
  • The legal basis for processing your data
  • How we protect your information
  • Your rights under the GDPR

By using our website or submitting your personal information, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2. Personal Data We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

We may collect personal information when you:

  • Book scuba diving sessions or tour packages
  • Submit an inquiry through our contact forms
  • Request pricing or travel information
  • Register for PADI scuba diving courses
  • Contact us via email, phone, or WhatsApp

This information may include:

  • Full Name & Email Address
  • Phone Number & Country of Residence
  • Travel Dates & Number of Participants
  • Emergency Contact Details (required for diving activities)
  • Booking Preferences & Documents voluntarily submitted

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP Address, Browser Type & Device Information
  • Operating System & Pages Visited
  • Time Spent on Website, Referral Source & Usage Statistics

2.3 Information from Third-Party Services

We may receive data from trusted third-party services such as:

  • Google Analytics & Google Ads
  • Social Media Platforms
  • Online Booking or Payment Providers

3. Why We Process Your Personal Data

We process your personal information to:

  • Confirm and manage scuba diving bookings
  • Arrange Lakshadweep tour packages
  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support
  • Process payments securely
  • Improve website performance and enhance user experience
  • Send booking confirmations
  • Send promotional offers (only with your consent)
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under GDPR, we process your personal data based on one or more of the following legal grounds:

  • Your Consent – You have given clear consent for us to process your personal data for a specific purpose (e.g., marketing emails).
  • Performance of a Contract – Processing is necessary to fulfil a booking or service agreement you have entered into with us.
  • Legitimate Business Interests – Processing is necessary for our legitimate interests, provided these are not overridden by your rights.
  • Compliance with Legal Obligations – Processing is required to comply with a legal obligation (e.g., government regulations in Lakshadweep).

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Improve website functionality and remember user preferences
  • Measure website traffic and analyze visitor behavior
  • Support advertising and marketing campaigns

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

6. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • SSL-encrypted website connections
  • Secure servers and hosting
  • Restricted access to customer data
  • Regular software and security updates
  • Secure data storage practices

Although we strive to protect your information, no internet transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted to our website.

7. Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell your personal data. Your information may only be shared with trusted third parties when necessary, including:

  • Payment service providers
  • Website hosting providers
  • Email communication services
  • Analytics providers (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Government authorities where legally required (e.g., Lakshadweep Administration for entry permits)

All third parties are expected to maintain appropriate privacy and security standards.

8. International Data Transfers

As Lak Sea Adventures operates from India, your personal information may be processed outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Whenever international transfers occur, we take appropriate safeguards to ensure your information remains protected in accordance with GDPR requirements, which may include:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission
  • Adequacy decisions where applicable
  • Other appropriate transfer mechanisms recognized under GDPR

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to:

  • Deliver requested services and complete bookings
  • Meet legal, tax, and regulatory obligations
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements
  • Improve our business operations

When personal data is no longer required, it will be securely deleted or anonymized.

10. Your GDPR Rights

If you are located in the EU or EEA, you have the following rights under GDPR:

Right to Access

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Right to Rectification

Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your data ("Right to be Forgotten").

Right to Restrict Processing

Limit how we use your personal data.

Right to Object

Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing.

Right to Data Portability

Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing.

Lodge a Complaint

Complain to your local Data Protection Authority (DPA).

We will respond to all GDPR requests within the time period required by law (generally within 30 days). To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 13 below.

11. Children's Privacy

Our scuba diving services and website are not intended for children under the age of 16 without parental or legal guardian consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a minor, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

12. Changes to This GDPR Policy

We may update this GDPR Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements, business practices, or website functionality. The latest version will always be published on this page with the effective date updated accordingly. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
