About Us

Our Story

Dive Into the Story of Lak Sea Adventure

Lak Sea Adventures is a Lakshadweep-based PADI Dive Resort founded by Lakshadweep's First Lady Dive Master and her instructor husband. With 13 years of experience in the field, they offer expert guidance, PADI certification, and local knowledge to create unforgettable diving experiences. As a PADI Dive Resort, they adhere to the highest standards of safety and quality.


Their team provides a range of services, including scuba diving, dive training, and PADI courses, catering to divers of all levels. From beginner courses like Open Water Diver to advanced certifications, they offer a variety of training programs. Customized packages are also available, tailored to suit individual needs and preferences.


Lakshadweep's stunning coral reefs and diverse marine life make it an ideal destination for diving enthusiasts. Lak Sea Adventures' passion for the ocean is evident in every dive, and they're dedicated to sharing the beauty of Lakshadweep's underwater world with their guests. With their expertise and commitment to safety, Lak Sea Adventures provides a unique and memorable experience for those looking to explore the ocean's depths. Whether you're a seasoned diver or just starting out, Lak Sea Adventures is the perfect partner for your Lakshadweep diving adventure.
